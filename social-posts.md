# Social launch posts

Announcing participation earns **+10 XP each** (X, LinkedIn, Instagram all count
separately). The requirement is simply that you post and tag **@lablabai**.

**Keep it honest.** You have joined and started building. You have not finished
anything yet. "I just started" is both accurate and what the program actually
wants to see — people announcing day one. Do not claim results you don't have.

Attach `cover.png` from the project root. It is 1200x630 and reads well in both
the X timeline and the LinkedIn feed.

## LinkedIn  (longer, tells the story)

```
I just joined the Lablab x AMD AI Academy Challenge, and I'm starting with a
problem I keep running into: OCR that falls apart on real-world images.

Demo datasets are clean. Reality is not. License plates shot at an angle,
motion blur at night, road signage half-obscured by glare — most OCR pipelines
shave characters off under those conditions and return confident nonsense.

So for my first build I'm putting a vision-language model on AMD ROCm and
holding it to a real budget: strict inference latency, a hard VRAM ceiling, and
correct handling of the jurisdiction banners printed around US plate numbers so
they don't contaminate the read. Chinese province characters preserved, not
translated away.

Two things I care about beyond the leaderboard:
- Everything open source, from day one.
- Real numbers, not vibes. If the pipeline breaks under glare, I want to see it
  break and then fix it.

The lablab team is tracking XP across a 3-month program, and the competition
is open to individual builders. If you're working on multimodal robustness,
I'd genuinely like to compare approaches — my team here is The HaloHubX.

#AMD #AIEngineering #ComputerVision #ROCm
```
